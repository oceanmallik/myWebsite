Privacy Policy

DIU Scholar

Welcome to DIU Scholar, a platform helping students out with their studies[cite: 2]. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and manage your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services to Daffodil Students[cite: 2], we collect and handle specific types of information:

  • Account Data: We handle user registration and authentication[cite: 3]. To restrict access, we strictly require registration with @diu.edu.bd emails[cite: 3].
  • Purchase Information: The app features a cart system to order courses[cite: 2]. Our database tracks course buyers by utilizing a unique #courseID that gets assigned to users after purchase[cite: 3].
  • Usage and Progress Data: We track course progress for users[cite: 3]. Every page has an icon to mark as read, which is tied to user accounts and managed by the database[cite: 4].

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to operate and improve the app experience:

  • To provide a personalized enrollment based calendar on the Home Tab[cite: 2].
  • To display your statistics and course progress on the My Profile page[cite: 2].
  • To allow admins the power to manually assign courses to students[cite: 3].
  • To help users order courses using the courseID, which is assigned to the respectable user upon database approval[cite: 2].

3. Third-Party Hosting and Services

DIU Scholar relies on external services to host app content and infrastructure:

  • The mobile app codebase and root repository information (in JSON format) are hosted on our GitHub organization[cite: 2].
  • Every course has its own project repository on GitHub containing content, PDFs, and quiz files[cite: 5].
  • Content videos for the courses are hosted on YouTube[cite: 2].

4. User Rights and Data Deletion

We believe in giving you control over your account data:

  • The My Profile page gives users the option to delete their profile entirely[cite: 2].
  • Users have the ability to archive courses after finishing them from the enrolled courses section[cite: 2].

5. Contact Information

If you have any inquiries regarding your data, or if you need to utilize the feature to contact a tutor[cite: 2], please reach out to us through the appropriate channels provided within the app.
